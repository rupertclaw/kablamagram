Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

The short version: Kablamagram has no accounts, no ads and never asks for your real name or email (challenges just use a nickname you choose). Your scores stay on your device. The game uses no cookies. We count anonymous usage statistics to see how the game is played and make it better, and you can switch that off below.

Saved on your device

Your high scores, longest word, words found online, chosen mode and settings (sound, whether you've seen the guide) are stored in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us. They stay until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to understand how the game is used. It records:

  • pages viewed, and whether the game was opened in a browser or as an installed app
  • game events: games started and finished, mode, score, the length of words played (not the words themselves), words the game didn't recognise or found online (so missing ones can be added to the dictionary), power tiles earned, shares, installs, and use of the help and sound buttons
  • device type, browser, operating system and screen size
  • approximate location (country and city), worked out from your IP address. Umami does not store IP addresses.

We don't collect names, emails or anything that identifies you directly. The data is used only to improve Kablamagram. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Cookies

Kablamagram uses no cookies. Umami counts visits without cookies and collects nothing that can recognise you, so there's nothing to accept. Your game progress is kept in your browser's local storage (see above), which never leaves your device.

Challenge links

When you challenge a friend, the link you send holds the board, your score and the nickname you typed. It goes only to whoever you share it with; we don't store it. When the link is opened, the game reads it and removes it from the address straight away, before any analytics loads, so your nickname is never sent to our analytics. Your nickname is remembered on your device for next time.

Daily leaderboard

The leaderboard is optional. If you join it, the game sends your nickname, your daily score and a random code made by the game on your device (so you can only appear once a day) to our leaderboard, which runs on Cloudflare. It stores only those, along with the puzzle number and the time. No email, real name or IP address is stored. Everyone can see the nicknames and scores on the day's top 20. Entries are deleted automatically after 30 days. To remove yourself sooner, open the leaderboard in the game and tap Remove me from the leaderboard: it deletes all your entries straight away. If you've lost access to the device you played on, email kablamagram@gmail.com with the nickname and the puzzle number. If you don't join, nothing is sent.

Checking words online

The game has a large dictionary built in. If you play a word of 4 or more letters that it doesn't know and you're online, it looks the word up on Wiktionary. Only the word itself is sent, nothing about you. Words found this way are remembered on your device so they work offline too.

Other services

  • Wiktionary (run by the Wikimedia Foundation) is used to check unknown words: see the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare runs the daily leaderboard: see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Umami (cookie-free analytics): see Umami's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Kablamagram is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information about them.
