Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026

The short version: Kablamagram has no accounts, no ads and never asks for your name or email. Your scores stay on your device. We count anonymous usage statistics to see how the game is played and make it better, and you can switch that off below.

Saved on your device

Your high scores, longest word, chosen mode and settings (sound, swapping, whether you've seen the guide) are stored in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us. They stay until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the game is used. It records:

  • pages viewed, and whether the game was opened in a browser or as an installed app
  • game events: games started and finished, mode, score, the length of words played (not the words themselves), power tiles earned, shares, installs, and use of the help and sound buttons
  • device type, browser, operating system and screen size
  • approximate location (country and city), worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store IP addresses.

We don't collect names, emails or anything that identifies you directly. The data is used only to improve Kablamagram. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga) so it can tell one visit from another. If you're in the EU/EEA, the UK or Switzerland, no analytics cookies are set: Google only receives basic, cookieless signals that can't recognise you on a later visit.

Other services

Children

Kablamagram is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information about them.
